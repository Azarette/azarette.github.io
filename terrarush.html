TerraRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

TerraRush is developed by Azarette ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the Android game TerraRush (com.azarette.terrarush) handles, why it is handled, and the choices available to you.

TerraRush is a single-player game. It does not require an account, a login, a name, an email address, a phone number, a precise location or access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Progress is stored locally on your device. The game shows banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and Google's advertising technology may process limited device and advertising information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

TerraRush stores game progress and settings locally in the app's private storage: coins, diamonds and collected rewards; owned bikes, paint/skins, upgrades and the selected bike; best distance, run count, total distance and lifetime coins; music/SFX volume, control mode and tutorial state.

This information is used only to run the game and restore your progress. It is not sent to us and is not linked to an account. You can delete it from Options → DELETE PROGRESS, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

2. Information processed by Google AdMob

TerraRush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to display a banner ad and to manage advertising consent. According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK and its advertising partners may process:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier) and similar device or app identifiers;
  • device information such as device model, operating-system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be derived;
  • ad impressions, clicks and interactions with the banner;
  • diagnostic and fraud-prevention information related to ad delivery;
  • your consent choices and privacy preferences.

We do not receive your name, email address, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, files, calendar, health data, financial information, messages, microphone audio or camera images through the game. We do not ask you to sign in.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's privacy policy. See also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

3. How the information is used

The advertising information is used to request, display and measure banner advertising; select personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on your consent and device settings; limit ad frequency, detect invalid traffic and prevent fraud; and comply with legal obligations and Google's advertising policies.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not use your game progress for advertising profiles.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device where consent is required (you can withdraw or change consent at any time); legitimate interests for non-personalized advertising, security, fraud prevention and keeping the game functional, balanced against your rights; and legal obligation where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

5. Your advertising choices

In the game

On first launch in a region where consent is required, Google's UMP consent form is shown. After that, open Options → PRIVACY SETTINGS to review or change your choice. The choice is stored by Google's UMP SDK on your device, so you are not asked again on every launch.

On your device

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad personalization in Android settings, typically under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer). You can also use Google's Ad Settings.

California and other US state privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know, delete, correct or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. You can exercise advertising choices through Options → PRIVACY SETTINGS, through the Android advertising-ID controls, or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

6. Sharing

We share advertising information with Google LLC and its advertising partners through the Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a corporate transaction. We do not share your local game progress with third parties.

7. Retention

Local progress remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google retains advertising and consent information according to its own retention policies. Consent choices are retained by the UMP SDK so that the game does not repeatedly ask the same question.

8. Children

TerraRush is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The AdMob request configuration explicitly marks the app as not child-directed and as not directed to users under the age of consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Security

Advertising requests use HTTPS. Game progress is stored in the app's private sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we control. Google is responsible for the security of the advertising SDK and its own systems.

10. International transfers

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own, including the United States, using the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy. The game itself does not transfer your local progress internationally.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because the game does not have accounts, we may not be able to identify you in Google's advertising data; in that case, Google's own privacy controls are the most direct way to exercise those rights.

To make a request or ask a privacy question, contact Azarette — Privacy at privacy@azarette.example.

12. Third-party links

This policy links to Google websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not apply to them.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, advertising configuration or legal requirements change. The updated version will show a new "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be reflected in the game or store listing where appropriate.
